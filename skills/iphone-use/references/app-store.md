# Installing and deleting apps

Observed on iPhone 14 Pro, iOS 26.4.1, App Store in India, 2026-11-03. Installing changes the phone and can cost money: the user approves each install, and only the user approves a purchase.

## Find the right app

1. `open_app` `App Store`, then `tap_text` `Search` (the tab at the bottom right).
2. Tap the search field at the top, type the app's name, `press_key` `enter`, `wait` about 2 s.
3. Read the results with care:
   - Results marked **Ad** come first and are often a different app (an Instagram ad for "TikTok").
   - **Lookalikes** sit high in results: "TikTok" in India returned "Tik Saver Pro Video Repost" by another developer, with a TikTok-style icon.
4. `tap_text` the app's **name** (the text, not the button) to open its listing. The listing shows the name, the **developer** under it, ratings, age rating and chart; Information further down has size and compatibility.
5. The app is the right one only when the **developer** matches the real publisher. If it isn't in the results, say it's not available in this store, and leave lookalikes alone.

## The button tells you the state

| Button | Means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Get** | Free, never downloaded on this Apple Account | Final action: ask first. Then iOS shows a confirmation (Face ID, side-button double-click or password): a user-only screen |
| Cloud icon | Downloaded before on this account | Final action: ask first. It installs at once, with no confirmation |
| **Open** | Installed | Nothing to install |
| A price (`₹ 599`) | Paid | User-only: hand over, never tap |

"In-App Purchases" under Get means a free app with paid extras.

## Install

1. On the listing, show the user the name, developer, price and size, and get a yes.
2. Tap the button. A spinner, then a progress ring, then **Open** means installed (a 377 MB app took about 10 s on Wi-Fi).
3. Open it with `open_app` and the icon label, which may be shorter than the store name: "Apple Support" installs as **Support**. `open_app`'s "similar names" message gives the label.
4. A first launch often shows a welcome, consent or permission screen. Show it to the user; consent is theirs to give.

## Delete an app

1. Open Spotlight (tap the Search pill on the Home Screen), type the icon label, `wait` about 1.5 s.
2. `long_press` the app's icon in **Top Hit** (1000 ms). The menu shows Share App, Require Face ID and **Delete App**.
3. Tap **Delete App**. The alert reads `Delete "<App>"? Deleting this app will also delete its data.` with Cancel and Delete.
4. Final action: show the alert and get a yes, then tap **Delete**.
5. Verify: `open_app` with that name now reports it isn't installed.
